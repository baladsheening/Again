# Again — the product truth

One page. If any other document disagrees with this one, this one wins.
Written 11 September 2026. `consumer-product-strategy.md` is the long form;
`view.md` is the outside read that produced it.

## The sentence

> **Again turns the private things I want to do into real-world plans when
> someone I trust wants the same thing.**

## Who it is for

People who already have trusted relationships — friends, a partner, a
household, colleagues, a small community — and who keep noticing things they
might want to do, see, try, learn, visit or buy, and keep losing them.

## The loop

> worldly cue → instant capture → shared relevance → one-tap real-world action
> → a better next capture

Every step is load-bearing. The product is not finished at step two.

## What makes it different

Notes stores the thought. Pinterest and Instagram help you find it. WhatsApp
helps you arrange it. **Again is the only one that notices two people
independently wanted the same thing, and says so at a moment either of them can
act on.** It is a possibility network, not a social network: what travels
between people is something somebody might do — never a post, a status, or an
audience.

## The payoff

A convergence has to produce an **opportunity**, not a notification. Two people
both wanting pottery is a coincidence; *Want to try this together?*, written and
ready to send, is the product. Again builds no chat, calendar, RSVP or payment
— it hands over the sentence and gets out of the way.

## The promise about privacy

Your record is yours and nobody reads it. What a mutual connection creates is
the possibility of a **one-line overlap**: the thing you both wrote, shown to
the person you both added, and nothing else. You are told that in plain words
before it can happen, and any single capture can be held out of it with one
control you can find.

## The launch wedge

One named, dense, already-connected community, in one context where people
actually act together. Not "everybody who wants things." The mechanic needs
people who already overlap; a wide catalogue is not a substitute for that.

## Anti-goals

No feed. No likes, comments, followers, scores or streaks. No engagement
metrics, and no opening counts. No stranger matching before the safety work
exists. No marketplace, checkout, affiliate revenue or retailer ranking. No
claim of complete coverage. No guessing at what somebody meant. **Nothing inside
the loop is ever paid for.**

## How we will know it worked

Somebody saved something. Somebody they trust independently wanted the same
thing. **The two of them had a conversation that would not otherwise have
happened.** Opens, notification clears and time spent are not measures of
anything.
